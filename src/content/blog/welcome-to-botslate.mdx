# A place for work in progress

Botslate is a small independent space for software projects, experiments, useful tools, and technical notes.

The idea is simple: make things, learn from the process, and document what is worth keeping. Some ideas may become durable tools. Others may remain focused experiments. Both can be useful when their purpose and limits are clear.

## What to expect

Notes here will stay close to real work. They may cover:

- small software experiments;
- focused utilities;
- web projects and interface details;
- implementation choices and lessons learned.

This site is built with Astro and published as a static website. That keeps the public surface straightforward while leaving room for writing, a changelog, search, and small interactive touches.

Botslate is intentionally modest. There are no invented claims or business metrics here—only projects, experiments, and notes as they take shape.
